# Why this target: research and the six scores

Checked 8 November 2026.

Outreach is a sales engagement platform: sequences of emails, calls and tasks for outbound sales teams, with a task queue for each rep, sending from the rep's mailbox, a dialler and reporting. Salesloft is its closest rival.

**Price.** Outreach does not publish prices; it sells annual per-seat contracts on a quote. Buyer-report sites put the entry plan near US$100 a user a month and higher tiers above that, with calling and other modules as add-ons ([CostBench](https://costbench.com/software/ai-sales-tools/outreach), [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/saas-insights/outreach-pricing)). These are third-party estimates, not vendor prices, and no savings claim is made from them.

**Export.** Outreach documents a CSV export of prospect records with all prospect fields, last touch, engagement score and sequence state ([Outreach help](https://support.outreach.io/support/solutions/articles/159000426222)).

| Score | 1 to 5 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Annual bill | 3 | Per-seat annual contracts with add-ons; quoted privately. |
| Data-shaped | 4 | Prospects, sequences, steps, touches and meetings are tables and a daily queue. Sending and the dialler are integrations. |
| Our buyers pay for it | 4 | Business-to-business sales teams in New Zealand and Australia run outbound; none confirmed in our records. |
| Search demand | 4 | "Outreach alternative" and "Salesloft alternative" are well-searched, and page one is mostly listicles. |
| One-session buildable | 4 | The records, queue, compliance and drafting are; automatic sending is out of scope. |
| Import path | 4 | Documented CSV export of prospects. |

Total 23 of 30. New family: sales engagement.
