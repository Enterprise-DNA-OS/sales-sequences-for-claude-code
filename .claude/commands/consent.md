---
description: Record why we may email a prospect: express, inferred or deemed consent, with a note of the evidence.
---

```bash
npm run seq -- set consent <prospect> --basis=express|inferred|deemed --note="the evidence" --actor="<operator>"
```

Read docs/compliance.md. Express: they asked or agreed. Inferred: an existing business relationship or conduct that shows they would expect it. Deemed (New Zealand): their address is conspicuously published for their work role and the message relates to that role. If the operator cannot say what the evidence is, do not record one: leave the email steps on hold.

Every answer starts from the CLI output. Use the record codes it prints. Never answer about a prospect, account or rep from memory, and never invent a record or a reply. If a name matches more than one record, show the candidates and ask. Nothing here sends: drafts go to drafts/ and a person sends them from their own mailbox.
